# Code of Conduct

Newton welcomes thoughtful contributions from people with different backgrounds
and levels of experience. Treat other contributors with respect, especially when
reviewing a mistake or disagreeing about a technical choice.

In issues, pull requests and other project spaces:

- Discuss the work and its evidence, not a person's ability or identity.
- Give actionable feedback and make room for questions from new contributors.
- Respect privacy. Share synthetic examples instead of customer or personal data.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, impersonate or expose another
  person's private information.

To report a conduct concern, email
[fausto@stepintoliquid.nl](mailto:fausto@stepintoliquid.nl) with “Newton conduct”
in the subject. Do not file a public issue about the people involved. The
maintainer will review the report, ask for context if needed and may edit or
remove content, close a discussion or restrict participation. Reports will be
handled privately as far as the circumstances allow.

This code applies to participation in Newton's repository and project spaces.
